# Text spec

Governed by [MASTER-CONTEXT.md](MASTER-CONTEXT.md).

## Audience

Naturinfo is primarily for adults. Writing should be engaging and easy to read, not academic.

Normal species descriptions, facts, and documentary narration must not be childified. Do not adopt the prototype chat voice — short playful asides, magic, or emoji — for page text or narration.

Quiz and Ask the Species may use simpler, child- and family-friendly language. That exception is specified in [AI-GUIDE-SPEC.md](AI-GUIDE-SPEC.md). Documentary narration is specified in [AUDIO-VIDEO-SPEC.md](AUDIO-VIDEO-SPEC.md) and stays in the adult register.

## Language

Swedish is the authoritative production master for text. Where English or German text already exists, migrate it with the species. It is existing translation content, not a second master, and not automatically a verified or final translation. See [LOCALISATION.md](LOCALISATION.md).

## Facts

Apply the master-context geographic rules to every sentence that states a fact.

Verify species, morphology, habitat, season, behaviour, and surrounding vegetation against the Kustvägen context: Nordanstig and Hälsingland, the adjacent Baltic and Bothnian coast, and Västernorrland first, and only ecologically appropriate nearby southern Norrland when that is genuinely the species' context.

Where it is biologically relevant, describe the species as it occurs in that area. That includes regional occurrence, a documented subspecies or population difference, seasonal plumage or coat, size or characteristics of the local population, habitat, coastal or freshwater context for fish and other aquatic species, and migratory or seasonal presence for birds. General correctness for Sweden is not enough when the local situation differs and that difference is documented.

Do not invent a local difference, a subspecies, a measurement, or a seasonal claim in order to make a text sound more local. If the sources do not support a regional distinction, write the established fact and do not add one.

Do not introduce North American or other foreign lookalikes, exotic vegetation, or invented measurements, behaviour, or folklore presented as fact.

When uncertain, verify first. Do not invent a fact to complete a paragraph. If an existing Naturinfo text and an older customer source disagree, follow the content-authority rule in [QA-AND-PUBLISHING.md](QA-AND-PUBLISHING.md).

## Page text versus other text

- Introduktion, Snabbfakta, and Fördjupning are written species content.
- Documentary narration is a separate listening script. Do not generate it by reading the page aloud, and do not replace the page text with the script.
- Public headings may differ from CMS section names and may be overridden per species. CMS names are listed in [CONTENT-LEVELS.md](CONTENT-LEVELS.md).
- Image alt text and captions describe the actual image and stay factually consistent with the species text.
- Public source lists are not required. Internal source references are. See [QA-AND-PUBLISHING.md](QA-AND-PUBLISHING.md).
